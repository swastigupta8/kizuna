What should be in the file?
Any normal docker-compose.yml works — Kizuna reads your services and depends_on to build the architecture graph. But plain compose files don't say anything about circuit breakers or timeouts, so without a little more detail every service defaults to "no protection detected." Add a few kizuna.* labels to any service to tell it what's actually there: 
services:
  payment-service:
    image: myorg/payment-service
    depends_on:
      - database
    deploy:
      replicas: 3
    labels:
      - "kizuna.critical=true"
      - "kizuna.circuit_breaker=true"
      - "kizuna.timeout_ms=2000"
No labels at all still works — you'll just see a lower score made up mostly of "nothing's configured yet" findings, which is honest, just maybe not what you meant to show off.
